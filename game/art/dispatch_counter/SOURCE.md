# Dispatch counter furniture

Kay Lousberg created these models and their shared texture for KayKit Furniture Bits 1.0. They are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. The author's notice is included in [LICENSE.txt](LICENSE.txt).

The three glTF files, their binary buffers and the PNG are unmodified copies from the [upstream glTF directory at commit 96d5930](https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Furniture-Bits-1.0/tree/96d5930a8dbdb363409bbc2d3341718b00e17c9c/addons/kaykit_furniture_bits/Assets/gltf). Each model resolves its texture and geometry from the same directory.
