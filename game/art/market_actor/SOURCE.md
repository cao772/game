# Market stall actor

The body and hood come from Kay Lousberg's CC0 KayKit Adventurers 1.0, at revision `672074b73ba276876a19e8816ecdc5241817ab47`. The original license accompanies this model.

The derivative keeps the unarmed, hooded body and embedded palette texture. A new russet work apron, ochre waist tape and patch pocket attach to the hips. The original weapons and cape were removed. The three clips are Idle, Interact and Cheer, for use with the shared counter actor controller. At its scale of 0.84 the bind-pose body is roughly 1.89 world units tall.

The original GLB is 3,597,652 bytes. This self-contained GLB is 346,212 bytes and has seven meshes and 41 joints. A static comparison checked retained mesh attributes, joint bindings, animation samplers and targets, and texture payload against the source. The new apron geometry has unit hip weights. Import and pose appearance await engine verification.
