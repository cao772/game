# Records clerk

Kay Lousberg created the original CC0 Rogue in KayKit Adventurers 1.0. This derivative uses the unhooded head and six skinned body meshes. The weapon and cape attachments were removed to give the clerk empty hands and a shorter silhouette.

The self-contained GLB includes the original palette texture, the 41-joint skin and the Idle, Interact and Cheer clips. Its foot origin and forward direction (+Z) follow the original asset. The standing body is roughly 1.84 units high at the counter actor's scale of 0.84.

The source GLB is 3,616,284 bytes. The reduced file is 360,340 bytes. Static inspection compared the retained mesh attributes, joint bindings, animation samples and targets, and embedded PNG with the original GLB. Godot import and animation playback await engine verification.
